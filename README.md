**I build internal tools and workflow systems with clear evidence, human review, and practical decision support.**

A Copenhagen-based DTU MSc Engineering graduate in Mathematical Modelling and Computation, building practical systems for complex operational work. My strongest work sits around internal tools, workflow automation with human review, data-quality workflows, and decision-support systems.

On the side I design and ship small Apple apps, alone, end to end.

### <picture><source media="(prefers-color-scheme: dark)" srcset="assets/heading-work-dark.svg"><img alt="Selected work" src="assets/heading-work-light.svg" height="40"></picture>

**[OpsDesk](https://jobops.zhangjiajia.me/opsdesk)** ↗ &nbsp;<sub>DEMO</sub><br>
A support/operations demo that turns cases into evidence, draft actions, approvals, feedback memory, and replayable logs.

**[Denmark Flex Planner](https://flex.zhangjiajia.me)** ↗ &nbsp;<sub>LIVE</sub><br>
A map-first decision-support tool for prioritizing Danish EV charging and local flexibility investments under budget, grid-zone, and regional constraints.

**[Afgang](https://me.isjia.page/en/projects/afgang)** &nbsp;<sub>CASE STUDY</sub><br>
A local-first Copenhagen transport utility combining commute decisions with conservative, timetable-based reachability maps.

**[ASCII Studio](https://ascii.isjia.page)** ↗ &nbsp;<sub>LIVE · [SOURCE](https://github.com/isjiajia01/ascii-studio)</sub><br>
Turns any image or video into ASCII, braille, halftone, dither or brick textures, rendered live in the browser and exported without uploading anything.

**[DTU × Mover Thesis](https://github.com/isjiajia01/dtu-thesis-workspace)** &nbsp;<sub>THESIS</sub><br>
A real logistics planning project: decide how multi-day deliveries should be admitted, routed, and recovered when depots are under stress.

**[Apple apps](https://app.isjia.page)** ↗ &nbsp;<sub>APP STORE</sub><br>
Eight small Apple apps designed and built solo, five live on the App Store for iPhone, Watch, TV, Mac and Safari. Local-first, no accounts, ads or tracking.
